## This document states how AI was used in building Ariami

AI has been used extensively throughout the project and helped take Ariami to where it is today.. Without it, Ariami wouldn't be close to where it is as I would have probably given up with the complexity that this project has.

Ariami has been actively developed since November 2025. During that time, I have been using it as much as possible, and at the time of writing this, Ariami has been my main music player, replacing Spotify, for 2 months, and I do not plan on looking back. I have tested it across clean virtual machines, computers, phones and real televisions, and have continued fixing problems and refining the experience as I saw fit. Ariami has been my companion on two holidays during that time, 

AI is amazing and it can generate and help debug code, but it genuinely cannot perform months of actual use and it also cannot be the one to decide when something is good to release. 

If Ariami contains bugs, poor decisions, or anything else that needs fixing, please blame me—not the AI or any particular model. I decide what gets built, which direction the project takes, what changes are accepted, and what gets released. I take responsibility for the result.

Ariami is still actively being developed, and there is more I want to do with it because I genuinely prefer using it to Spotify.

If the extensive use of AI is a deal-breaker for you, no offence is taken.
